What this needs

A TikTok developer app at developers.tiktok.com with Login Kit and the Content Posting API, asking for the scopes user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list and video.upload. Put the client key and secret in .env as TIKTOK_CLIENT_KEY and TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET.

An upload arrives as a draft in your TikTok inbox for you to caption and post — the inbox endpoint has no caption field, so the post copy is written to the log for you to paste. Posting straight to the profile needs video.publish and TikTok's audit, and until that passes TikTok restricts everything an unaudited client posts to private viewing. This tool does not try to get around it. TikTok also allows at most 5 pending inbox shares in a rolling 24 hours.
