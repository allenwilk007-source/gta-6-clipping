What this needs

A Professional (Business or Creator) Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page you administer, plus a Meta app using Instagram API with Facebook Login and the permissions instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list and pages_read_engagement. Put the app id and secret in .env as INSTAGRAM_APP_ID and INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET.

The Facebook Page is not a formality. The other flavour of the API, Instagram Login, publishes only from a public video_url that Meta's servers fetch — and nothing here is on the public internet. Only the Facebook Login flavour accepts the bytes from this machine. Instagram publishes for real, with no draft step, and allows 50 posts in a moving 24 hours.
